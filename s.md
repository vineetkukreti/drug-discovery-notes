## 🇩🇪 GERMANY — The DiGA Goldmine

Germany is the **most exciting market** for a healthcare AI builder because of one unique thing:

### The DiGA (Digital Health Application) Framework

Germany's Digital Healthcare Act allows **digital health apps to be prescribed by doctors and fully reimbursed by health insurance**. This means if you build a health AI app, German insurance companies will PAY for it per patient. This is recurring revenue.

**How it works:** You build an AI health tool (e.g., mental health chatbot, chronic disease management, clinical decision support). You apply for the BfArM Fast-Track process. Once approved, your app gets listed in the DiGA directory. German doctors can then prescribe it, and insurance pays you directly. Prices range from €200 to €700+ per patient per quarter.

**Recent update (Feb 2026):** Germany's Federal Ministry of Health just adjusted DiGA rules again, making it more accessible. The market is growing fast.
